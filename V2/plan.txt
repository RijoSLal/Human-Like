V1 was focusing on embedded systems, the goal was to create a robot conversation system. 
V2 will be focusing on advanced human-level conversation, where besides having seamless STT, TTS, LLM with VAD and acoustic echo cancellation like in V1, 
V2 will be focusing on an AI conversation system via call or web, multi-user session based rather than one-to-one like V1. 
You don't need AEC because WebRTC has built-in AEC, or WebSocket will have negligible issues with acoustic echo (microphone picking up what the speaker played). 
V2 will also contain GMM + wav2vec clustering based emotion and gender recognition in real time, 
with voice based call endings and "helloing" if the user speaks something and waits too long, etc, making V2 as close to human calls as possible. 
The main challenge I am undertaking is that V2 should be fully open source, no APIs allowed, and everything should fit under 4GB VRAM
